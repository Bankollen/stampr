Using Stampr

Stampr is a tool for adding watermarks to PDF documents and removing watermarks from them. You may only use it on documents you own or are allowed to modify. Do not use Stampr to remove watermarks that protect someone else's rights.

Free and Pro

The free version can be used without an account or limits on how often you use it. Stampr Pro unlocks additional features and is sold either as a yearly subscription or as a one-time lifetime license. A license is for one person and can be activated in the number of browsers stated at checkout. "Lifetime" means for as long as Stampr is offered.
