Privacy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Your files stay on your device

Stampr works entirely in your web browser. The PDFs, fonts and images you choose are read and processed on your own device and are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

No ads, no tracking, no cookies

Stampr shows no ads, uses no analytics or tracking tools and sets no cookies.

What is stored in your browser

Your watermark settings, any saved profiles and — if you have Pro — your license key are kept in your browser's local storage so they are there next time. They never leave your device except as described below, and you can delete them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Services we rely on

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when you load the page. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Cloudflare provides DNS for the domain only; your page visits do not pass through Cloudflare.
  • Lemon Squeezy handles Pro purchases as the merchant of record. When you buy, you give your payment and contact details to Lemon Squeezy, not to us; we receive your name, email address and order details. When Pro is activated or re-checked, your browser sends your license key and a short device label (such as "Chrome on Windows") to Lemon Squeezy. See the Lemon Squeezy privacy policy.
