Privacy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Your files stay on your device

Stampr works entirely in your web browser. The PDFs, fonts and images you choose are read and processed on your own device and are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

No ads, no tracking, no cookies

Stampr shows no ads, uses no analytics or tracking tools and sets no cookies.

Stampr shows no ads and sets no cookies. To know how many people use Stampr we count page visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies, doesn't follow you across sites and doesn't identify you — we only see totals such as the number of visits per day and country.

What is stored in your browser

Your watermark settings and any saved profiles are kept in your browser's local storage so they are there next time. They never leave your device except as described below, and you can delete them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Services we rely on

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when you load the page. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Cloudflare provides DNS for the domain; your page visits do not pass through Cloudflare. Cloudflare Web Analytics receives an anonymous, cookieless visit count from each page load. See the Cloudflare privacy policy.
